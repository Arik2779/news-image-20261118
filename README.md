Entry for [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty "Sum up 18 November's world news in one image" — see the bounty page: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118.

The program (`main.ts`, Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png`
to `/output/entry.png`. The image is a newspaper front page for Wednesday
18 November 2026: masthead "WORLD NEWS" with the date, and four illustrated
panels, each verified against reporting from 18 November 2026 (see
`../RESEARCH.md`):

1. **APEC OPENS IN SHENZHEN.** The 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting
   runs 18–19 November in Shenzhen, China; President Xi Jinping delivers
   the keynote at the APEC CEO Summit opening ceremony on the morning of
   18 November and chairs the leaders' meeting.
2. **TRUMP LANDS IN SHENZHEN.** President Donald Trump arrives in Shenzhen
   late on Wednesday 18 November from Manila, the first sitting US
   president to land in the city. His bilateral meeting with Xi and the
   first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral are set for Thursday 19 November.
3. **NVIDIA BEATS: $115.8 BILLION.** Nvidia reported Q3 revenue of $115.8
   billion, up 103% year on year and above the $109.1B analyst estimate,
   with data centre revenue of $107.6B and Q4 guidance of $130B — its
   first results since Jensen Huang resigned as CEO on 12 November. The
   release came at 4:20 p.m. ET on 17 November, i.e. 04:20 WIB on
   18 November, so it was first reported in Asia on the morning of
   18 November.
4. **CYCLONE AKVAN TURNS SEVERE.** Cyclone Akvan over the Bay of Bengal
   becomes a Severe Cyclonic Storm around the morning of Wednesday
   18 November, with winds of 100–110 km/h gusting to 120; landfall is
   forecast for Friday 20 November on India's Andhra Pradesh coast.

The image is a JPEG in a file named `entry.png`, which the rules allow
(the format is told apart by its first bytes). 1024×1024 px, 264,474 bytes
(under the 1 MiB limit).

Entry for [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty "Sum up 18 November's world news in one image" — see the bounty page: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118.

This is v2: a rebuild from a re-research of the FULL day of 18 November 2026
(v1 was built at ~00:30 UTC on 18 Nov and could only verify the day's first
~30 minutes). The four panels are the day's most consequential stories
across four regions and fields — see `../RESEARCH-v2.md`.

The program (`main.ts`, Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png`
to `/output/entry.png`. The image is a newspaper front page for Wednesday
18 November 2026: masthead "WORLD NEWS" with the date, a large hero panel
and three panels below, each verified against reporting from 18 November
2026:

1. **IRAN STRIKES KUWAIT & BAHRAIN.** Early on 18 November, Iran launched
   strikes against Kuwait and Bahrain, saying it targeted Kuwait's Ali Al
   Salem Air Base, which hosts US forces, after the US struck Qeshm Island
   on 17 November. Kuwait engaged drones; Bahrain said it intercepted
   Iranian attacks.
2. **XI MEETS PUTIN IN SHENZHEN.** Chinese President Xi Jinping and
   Russian President Vladimir Putin held bilateral talks in Shenzhen around
   midday on 18 November, on the sidelines of the 33rd APEC summit. Both
   addressed the APEC CEO Summit that day — Xi against trade protectionism,
   Putin against Western sanctions. The first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral
   is set for Thursday 19 November and had not happened yet.
3. **PELTOLA WINS ALASKA.** Democrat Mary Peltola defeated two-term
   Republican Sen. Dan Sullivan, giving Democrats a 56–44 Senate majority —
   the last Senate race of the 2026 midterms to be called. Peltola took
   ~50.1% of first-choice votes, clearing the 50% threshold outright, and
   is the first Alaska Native elected to the US Senate. (AP call: Tuesday
   evening US time, i.e. Wednesday 18 Nov UTC; reported across the 18 Nov
   editions.)
4. **NVIDIA BEATS: $115.8 BILLION.** Nvidia reported Q3 revenue of $115.8
   billion, up 103% year on year and above the $109.1B analyst estimate,
   with data centre revenue of $107.6B and Q4 guidance of $130B — its
   first results since Jensen Huang resigned as CEO on 12 November. The
   release came at 4:20 p.m. ET on 17 November, i.e. 04:20 WIB on
   18 November, so it was first reported in Asia on the morning of
   18 November.
